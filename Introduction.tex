\GRPchapter{Introduction}

Gamma RP, or \GammaRP, is a tabletop roleplaying system meant to strike a balance between pure theater-of-the-mind systems and map based tabletop systems.

I've played many TTRPG systems, and I've found the most engaging systems for me were ones that had a visual element, but these by necessity often put a lot of burden of preparation on the moderator(s) of the game. They also can limit the amount of improvisation possible by the players.

I am a busy professional who daydreams a lot about worldbuilding. I wanted a system where any maps, items, or enemies could be very quickly created from scratch on-the-fly by an untrained game master.

So I spent a years' worth of free time creating \GammaRP so that I could play a campaign I titled ``Gamma Nebula'' with my friends.
