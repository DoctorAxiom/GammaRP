\section{The Fundamental Rolling Mechanic}

The fundamental rolling mechanic in \GammaRP is based on 12 sided dice. If you intend to play the game on a real physical tabletop, you may want to obtain a few dozen 12 sided dice.

Typically, the notation for a roll is written as D\textit{X}a\textit{Y}
