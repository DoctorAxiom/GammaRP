\GRPchapter{Augments}\label{chap:augments}

This chapter contains augments and attunements. Augments may often be left off quickly generated NPC's.

Augments and attunements may not be added or changed after character creation, except via the \emph{Enhance} downtime action.

Your character may only have one of each type of augment (or attunement) active. Duplicate augments or attunements are not allowed, even if any custom properties are different.

\section{Attunements}

Each Attunement takes 2 augment slots. All attunements have four tiers roughly as follows:

\begin{description}
	\item[Tier 0] Applies benefits at this tier as soon as the character takes the attunement.
	\item[Tier 1] Invest 1XP to gain all the Tier 1 benefits.
	\item[Tier 2] Invest 2XP to gain all Tier 2 benefits.
	\item[Tier 3] Invest 3XP to gain all Tier 3 benefits.
\end{description}
