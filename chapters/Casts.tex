\GRPchapter{Casts}\label{chap:casts}

Casts are generally divided into spells, charms, and hexes.

Spells are casts that produce immediate, momentary effects.

Charms are casts that produce lasting continuous effects.

Hexes are casts that sit dormant after casting until triggered, and then produce a momentary effect.

There is some overlap between these categories, and not all casts may fit neatly into one of these categories.

Casts are grouped in this chapter by the weight of their cast focus. More powerful casts have heavier cast foci.

The price of a cast focus is $100\times10^\GRPstat{Wt}$

All casts take one action to cast by default.

All casts have an associated talent you must roll for the cast to perform it's intended action. For Hexes, this roll may not occur right away but may only occur when the effect is triggered.

\section{Wt 0 Casts}

This section is all casts with a cast focus of wt 0. All of these are valid casts for your character to start with at character creation. Cast focuses with wt0 all cost 100 gold.

\begin{GRPtablewide}
	\caption{All Casts with a Wt 0 focus}

	\GRPcastcard{Agilify}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Insight}}
	{%
	The target of this spell gains one offensive action this phase (even if the target is currently in defensive phase). 

	In open mode the target gains \GRPaa{2} on the next talent roll they make within 10 seconds, as long as that talent roll has to do with moving quickly.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Analyze Material}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Perception}}
	{%
	Tells the caster information about what elements the object they are touching is made of. More successes reveal more elements within the composition of the object (and how they are arranged). The caster must be familiar with the elements in question, otherwise the spell just reveals that there is an unknown element.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Buffercast}{Spell}{Self}{\GRPtalent{Will}}
	{%
	Make a secondary cast with the same action (but consuming another cast use). The roll for the secondary cast and it's effects only occur at the start of your next phase, as long as your character is not incapacitated at that time. The number of successes on your secondary cast is taken to be the smaller of the number of successes between your buffercast roll and your secondary cast roll.

	In \emph{open} mode, buffercast delays the effects of your next cast by an amount of time between 10 and 30 seconds, chosen by your character at the time of casting.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Cordiality Charm}{Charm}{Room}{\GRPtalent{Charisma}}
	{%
	Applies to all characters in the current room. These characters recieve additional advantage equal to the number of successes on your cast roll. This charm lasts for 10 minutes or until the end of a phased mode scene, whichever comes first.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Deflection}{Hex}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Intellect} (delayed)}
	{%
	When the target next takes damage, roll your cast roll. Damage is reduced by the number of successes on your cast roll.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Fire Burst}{Spell}{Room}{\GRPtalent{Athletics}}
	{%
	Emit waves of fire from your character that can fill the room. Inflict elemental damage on everything in the room of any amount up to your number of successes on the cast roll. 
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Generate Noise}{Charm}{Self}{\GRPtalent{Memory}}
	{%
	Produce any sound your character is capable of imagining, as long as 10 minute in length or until the end of a phased mode scene. If trying to deceive another character about the source of the sound, contest your cast roll against the other character's \GRPtalent{Perception}. For longer sounds, the GM may rule multiple roll contests are needed to keep someone or something fooled.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Generate Image}{Charm}{Self}{\GRPtalent{Memory}}
	{%
	Produce a 3 dimensional moving image of anything your character is capable of imagining, which is visible for up to 10 minutes or until the end of a phased mode scene. If trying to deceive another character that the image is a real object, contest your cast roll against the other character's \GRPtalent{Perception}. For longer durations, the GM may rule multiple roll contests are needed to keep someone or something fooled.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Gravitate}{Spell}{Line-of-sight}{\GRPtalent{Fortitude}}
	{%
	Contest with the target's \GRPtalent{Fortitude}. If successful, either pull the target towards you the equivalent of 1 move action, or push the target away from you the equivalent of 1 move action.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Imbuecast}{Hex}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Insight} (delayed)}
	{%
	Make a secondary cast with the same action (but consuming another cast use). The effects of the secondary cast \textbf{and} your roll for it only occur when the target next makes an attack and if that attack is before the end of the next phase. The number of successes on your secondary cast is taken to be the smaller of the number of successes between your imbuecast roll and your secondary cast roll.

	The range of the secondary spell originates at the target of the attack, but the target of the secondary cast is still determined by you (the character who cast Imbuecast).

	If casting on an unwilling target contest your cast roll with the target's \GRPtalent{Fortitude} to deterine success.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Lightning Bolt}{Spell}{Line-of-sight}{\GRPtalent{Dexterity}}
	{%
	You shoot a bolt of lightning at your target, who takes elemental damage equal to the successes on your roll.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Reconstruct}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Intellect}}
	{%
	If you have a broken object in front of you that you fully understand the function and construction of, and you have the constituent elements of any missing components immediately available, you may restore this object to full function in a single action. The number of successes on your roll must be greater than or equal to the craft grade of the object being repaired.

	If the number of successes is not equal to the craft grade of the object being repaired, this spell destroys components of the object such that you must procure its parts again before retrying.

	If the object is not something your character created themselves, it is up to the GM if your character is familiar enough with the object to simply repair it, or if it will require a separate \GRPtalent{Memory} roll to see if your character would be have knowledge of it.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Redirect Magic}{Hex}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Charisma} (delayed)}
	{%
	Do not roll your cast roll right away. The next time the target makes a cast, you may contest their roll with your cast roll. If you succeed, you may change the target of their cast to a different target of your choice in the same room as you (including objects in the room).
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Telepathic Link}{Charm}{Room}{\GRPtalent{Will}}
	{%
	Establishes a telepathic link between a number of characters up to the number of successes on your cast roll, as long as they are in the same room as you at the time of casting.

	Telepathic Link lasts until next downtime unless dispelled.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Trace Magic}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Perception}}
	{%
	Tells the caster information about magic recently performed on the target or by the target. More successes reveal more information. Can tell you if the target is under the effects of a charm or hex.

	In general- the number of successes on the spell roll determine how many casts or effects on the target you get information about, and higher level casts or other magic may require more than 1 success per item to get information.
	}
\end{GRPtablewide}
