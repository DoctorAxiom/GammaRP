\GRPchapter{Casts}\label{chap:casts}

Casts are generally divided into spells, charms, and hexes.

\begin{itemize}
	\item[Spells] are casts that produce immediate, momentary effects.
	\item[Charms] are casts that produce lasting continuous effects.
	\item[Hexes] are casts that sit dormant after casting until triggered, and then produce a momentary effect.
\end{itemize}

Casts are grouped in this chapter by the weight of their cast focus. More powerful casts have heavier cast foci.

The price of a cast focus is $100\times10^\GRPstat{Wt}$

All casts have an associated talent you must roll for the cast to perform it's intended action. This cast roll occurs at the time of casting. All casts take one action to cast by default.

When casts are applying magic directly to an unwilling target, the target may resist with a castparry.

The process of a castparry is as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Note if the cast specifies a talent to parry with. If left unspecified, the talent is assumed to be \GRPtalent{Fortitude}.
	\item For cast effects that happen immediately- a castparry is equivalent to the target and caster entering a roll contest-- the caster's cast roll contested by the target's relevant talent. If the caster wins the contest- the successes on the last roll of the contest are their number of successes for the cast roll.
	\item For delayed cast effects, the target simply rolls their relevant talent and reduces the number of successes on the caster's cast roll by the number of successes (very much like an attack parry). If the number of successes is reduced to 0 or less, the cast has no effect.
\end{enumerate}

Castparries only apply when the magic is being directly applied to the target. Casts that produce an effect in the environment which must then be intepreted or resisted may require other talents to do so.

\section{Common Use Cases}

Bringing a deceased character back to life when the body is present-  conjure to replace any critical missing tissue, trace magic to find the character's soul residue within the ambient essentia, summon to bring the soul you are now aware of back into their body.

When the body is not present, you can conjure an approximate body made of ectoplasm and bring soul into that- this would create a ghost.

block casting in an area- reactivecast whenever a cast is made - secondary cast is instacast redirect magic into nothing.  You can Enchant to make it applicable multiple times.
