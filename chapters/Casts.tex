\GRPchapter{Casts}\label{chap:casts}

Casts are generally divided into spells, charms, and hexes.

Spells are casts that produce immediate, momentary effects.

Charms are casts that produce lasting continuous effects.

Hexes are casts that sit dormant after casting until triggered, and then produce a momentary effect.

There is some overlap between these categories, and not all casts may fit neatly into one of these categories.

Some casts ask for you to make a secondary cast- which allows you to chain together casts for more complex effects.

Casts are grouped in this chapter by the weight of their cast focus. More powerful casts have heavier cast foci.

The price of a cast focus is $100\times10^\GRPstat{Wt}$

All casts take one action to cast by default.

All casts have an associated talent you must roll for the cast to perform it's intended action. This cast roll occurs at the time of casting.

\section{Wt 2 Casts}

This section is all casts with a cast focus of wt 2. Your character may not start with these casts unless purchased with starting money. Cast focuses with wt2 all cost 10,000 gold.


\begin{GRPtablewide}
	\caption{All Casts with a Wt 2 Focus}

	\GRPcastcard[ENCH]{Enchant}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Insight}}
	{%
		Target any hex or charm within range.

		If targetting a hex, it may now be triggered multiple times before it expires- a number of times equal to the number of successes on your cast roll for Enchant. However any conditions, secondary casts, and the number of successes on the original hex are all locked and may not be changed by the original caster between repetitions.

		If targetting a charm- it repeats the exact effects it had when it was cast when it would otherwise expire. This repetition happens a number of additional times equal to the number of successes on your cast roll for Enchant.
	}

	\GRPcastcard[FABR]{Fabricate}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Intellect}}
	{%
		If you have a broken object in front of you that you fully understand the function and construction of, and you have the constituent elements of any missing components immediately available, you may restore this object to full function in a single action. The number of successes on your roll must be greater than or equal to the craft grade of the object being repaired.

		If the number of successes is not equal to the craft grade of the object being repaired, this spell destroys components of the object such that you must procure its parts again before retrying.

		If the object is not something your character created themselves, it is up to the GM if your character is familiar enough with the object to simply repair it, or if it will require a separate \GRPtalent{Memory} roll to see if your character would be have knowledge of it.

		You can also use this spell to apply previously crafted upgrades to equipment within a single action during phased mode- the number of successes for this must be equal to the craft grade of the final product, and on failure, the upgrade is destroyed and must be produced again.
	}

	\GRPcastcard[RCTN]{Reactionhex}{Hex}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Memory}}
	{%
		Choose any trigger as if you're choosing a trigger for a Defensive reaction in Defensive phase, with the restriction that the trigger must be something that occurs within the same room as the target.

		Cast a secondary cast. This secondary cast only goes into effect when your specified trigger for this cast happens. The target of the secondary cast may depend on the trigger, but any secondary effects must be chosen at the time of casting reactionhex. The number of successes on your secondary cast is whichever is smaller between your successes on this cast and your secondary cast roll.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Biomorph}{Charm}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Athletics}}
	{%
		Produce one of the following effects, targetting characters only:
		\begin{itemize}
	 		\item For every 2 successes on the cast roll, any living target gains 1 specific species ability of the caster's choice.
	 		\item For every 3 successes on the cast roll, the target has their body features altered to appear as if they are another species that the caster is familiar with.
	 		\item For every 4 successes on the cast roll, the target is fully transform species or gender to that of the caster's choice (it would take 8 successes to change species \textbf{and} gender).
	 	\end{itemize}

	 	These effects all last 10 minutes before expiring, and the target goes back to their previous state.

	 	If cast on an unwilling target, the caster must contest their cast roll with the target's \GRPtalent{Fortitude}. If successful, the number of successes on the caster's final roll count as the number of successes for this charm.

	 	In general, a GM should not allow a player character to be modified by any NPC's or other players without their consent.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Conjure}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Fortitude}}
	{%
		Produce a quantity of a specific form of an element from ambient Essentia. 

		\begin{itemize}
			\item For Primary elements (stone, water, air, metal)- you can conjure 100g per success on your cast roll
			\item For Noble elements (things necessary for life)- you can conjure 10g per success
			\item For Higher elements you can conjure 1g per success or less at the GM's discretion.
		\end{itemize}

		Your character must be familiar with the element to produce it, and your conjured element may be in any three dimensional shape you desire.

		You can use this to conjure void, but the effect is identical to the \GRPability{Implosion} spell.

		Of note- a single gold piece is 100 grams. This spell is not a convenient way to generate money.
	}

	\GRPcastcard[SUMN]{Summon}{Spell}{Awareness}{\GRPtalent{Charisma}}
	{%
		Instantly move any target you are aware of to a location of your choice within the current room.

		A target you can currently detect with any of your senses only takes 1 success to summon.

		A target you know about but cannot currently detect with your senses takes 1 additional success to summon.

		A distant target takes 1 additional success per kilometer of distance.
	}

	\GRPcastcard{Instacast}{Spell}{Touch}{\GRPtalent{Perception}}
	{%
		You must cast this immediately after another cast is made in the same room, or else there is no effect. Roll for this cast, and then roll for a secondary cast (consuming another cast use for your secondary cast). The secondary cast goes into effect at the same time as the last cast made in the same room. If the secondary cast is a hex or charm it goes into effect just before the previous caster finishes making their cast.

		The number of successes on your secondary cast is whichever is the smaller between your successes on this cast and your secondary cast roll.
	}

	\GRPcastcard[FSTP]{Feystep}{Spell}{Self}{\GRPtalent{Dexterity}}
	{%
		Your character steps through fey space, instantly teleporting a distance.

		Instantly take the equivalent of a number of move actions equal to the number of successes on your cast roll.

		When counting move actions, you may bypass obstructions and traps as if they are not there, and travel along visual only connectors on a map. Additionally, all connectors only take one move action to pass, even if they have arrows indicating traversing them normally would require extra move actions.
	}

	\GRPcastcard[PWRC]{Powercast}{Spell}{Self}{\GRPtalent{Will}}
	{%
		Roll your cast roll for powercast, then roll for a secondary cast a number of times up to the number of successes on Powercast. For each re-roll of the secondary cast you must also use consume a cast use (but you do not use additional actions to do so).

		The successes on all of your rolls for that secondary cast are added together and count as the number of successes for a single cast of that secondary cast.
	}

\section{Common Use Cases}

Bringing a deceased character back to life when the body is present-  conjure to replace any critical missing tissue, trace magic to find the character's soul residue within the ambient essentia, summon to bring the soul you are now aware of back into their body.

When the body is not present, you can conjure an approximate body made of ectoplasm and bring soul into that- this would 

block casting in an area- reactivecast whenever a cast is made - secondary cast is instacast redirect magic into nothing.  You can Enchant to make it applicable multiple times.
