\chapter{Gamma Runes}

Gamma runes are the most common runic system in the Gamma Nebula Universe. Casts are performed by channeling essentia along the shapes of gamma runes- which directly creates magical effects in the world. More runes and more complex effects need a larger amount of channeled essentia to actually create the desired effect.

Other runic systems exist, but the gamma rune system has been refined by arcanists over several millenia to account for almost all possible magical effects.

It is optional to learn Gamma Runes for gameplay. A GM who has learned Gamma Runes should not force any player to learn Gamma Runes.

Most situations can just be narrated vaguely during gameplay by saying the player found a loophole in a spell, or realized they could modify the runes to produce a certain effect. However, a GM and a player who have both learned Gamma Runes can include segments of gameplay where the player actually deciphers or debugs underlying runes.
