\GRPchapter{Building a Character}

Now that we understand the basics of die rolls in the \GammaRP{} system, we can walk through the process of building a character.

The same character building process is used for non-player-characters (NPC's)\index{NPC} and player characters (PC's)\index{PC}. Non-player characters built by the GM may have some details left off, and this chapter will indicate when something is nonessential for a non-player character.

\section{Fundamental Attributes}

All characters have 6 fundamental attributes that must be specified.

\noindent3 \emph{Foundations} - Mind, Body, and Heart

and

\noindent3 \emph{Characteristics} - Precision, Strength, Grace

The foundations and characteristics are combined to form 9 fundamental \emph{talents} that all characters have.
